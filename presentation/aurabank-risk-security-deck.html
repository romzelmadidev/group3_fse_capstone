    Security in layers, not in one place.

    Identity and sessions

    • Signed tokens. The caller comes from the token, never from the request body.
    • Refresh rotation. Refresh tokens are exchanged from a cookie and rotated.
    • Email code on first sign-in, with resend.
    • Device registry. List, approve, set primary, revoke.
    • Logout this device, other sessions, or everywhere.
    • Biometric unlock in the app for sign-in and transfers.

    Gateway rate limits

    • Sign-in and accounts: 10 requests a second, burst of 20.
    • Transfers: 20 a second, burst of 40, per user.

    Money integrity

    • Idempotency key. Tap four times, pay once.
    • Row locks taken in a fixed order, so transfers can't race.
    • Double entry. Every debit has its credit.
    • Outbox. The event commits with the money.
    • Saga rollback and a dead-letter queue for failures.

    Device and session signals

    RootedHookingEmulatorMock GPSAttestation failedScreen sharingAnyDesk runningOn a live callPasted payeePacket capture

    KYC vault

    • Private storage. Uploads go straight in over links that expire in 5 minutes.
    • Reviewers get read-only links.

    Compliance by construction

    • Immutable audit trail in PostgreSQL.
    • CTR filings at ₱500,000 a day.
    • SAR drafts written when risk blocks.
    • Maker-checker reversals. The checker can't be the maker.
    • Replay daemon drains the dead-letter queue.
    • Risk events appended to a JSONL log.

    Where we harden next.

    Holding today

    Escalate only. No injection memo has lowered a verdict.
    Server-side limits. The app warns early, the server decides, and failures close.
    Sub-millisecond memo reads, so Stage B fits the 200 ms payment budget.
    Every block leaves a trail: incident, audit entry, SAR draft.

    Real labels. Re-validate XGBoost on production outcomes. Today's benchmark is synthetic.
